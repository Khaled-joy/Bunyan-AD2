Build a single home page (one page only, no other routes, no backend) for Bunyan AD, an Abu Dhabi behavioral-science company that converts gameplay into psychometric data.

Stack: React + Vite + Tailwind CSS.
Look: dark theme — background 
#0B0F1A, text 
#F5F5F0, accent gold 
#C9A227. Headings in Space Grotesk, body in Inter. Large type, heavy whitespace, minimal chrome. Research-lab feel, not startup-SaaS. Fully responsive, mobile-first. Everything lives on this one scrolling page.

Content blocks, top to bottom on the home page:

1. Hero (full viewport)

Eyebrow, small caps, letter-spaced: ABU DHABI
H1: We turn play into evidence.
Sub: Bunyan AD converts gameplay into structured behavioral data.
Background: faint animated grid or slow-drifting particles, subtle, low opacity.
One outline button: How it works → smooth-scrolls to block 2.

2. The Trace

H2: Every game is a decision made under pressure.
Body: What to risk, when to wait, how to recover from a mistake, whether to cooperate or compete. Those decisions leave a trace. We instrument that trace — timing, hesitation, error correction, persistence after failure, strategy shifts — and convert it into structured behavioral data.

3. Why behavior, not self-report (two columns on desktop, stacked on mobile)

Left column, small heading SELF-REPORT: Most of what organizations know about people comes from asking them. Surveys depend on a person being willing to answer honestly, able to observe themselves accurately, and unaffected by how they want to be seen. Memory, mood, and social desirability shape every answer.
Right column, small heading BEHAVIOR: A player absorbed in a game does not decide to appear patient on move forty. They simply act — and the pattern of those actions is the signal. We measure what people do, repeatedly, under conditions we control.

4. What we measure

H2: What gameplay gives us
Six items in a responsive grid (3 across desktop, 2 tablet, 1 mobile), each a label + one line: Risk appetite · Learning rate · Attention stability · Response to setback · Planning horizon · Social orientation.
Closing line under the grid, centered, larger: Captured continuously — not at a single point in time, and free of language and cultural bias.

5. Closing

Centered, large: Not what someone says about themselves. What they demonstrate.
Below it, smaller, gold: Bunyan AD — Games in. Insight out.
